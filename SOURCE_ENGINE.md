# Source-of-law classification engine

This file records the locked algorithmic architecture for the Source / nature-of-law result zone. Public explanatory copy can change later without changing this taxonomy.

## Public nouns

The classifier may publicly return:

- **Positivist**
- **Natural Lawyer**
- **Interpretivist**
- **Fullerian**
- **Non-Positivist** only when a distinctive nonpositivist subtype is affirmatively established below the noun layer. A merely nonpositive Source score is not enough.

The following remain internal/legacy compatibility records rather than public nouns:

- Inclusive Positivist
- Exclusive Positivist
- Dworkinian
- Moral Impact Theory
- Radbruchian / Extreme-Injustice theory

## Modifier layer

### Positivist

Substantive modifiers:

- **Inclusive Positivist** — a source-grounded legal system may incorporate genuine moral criteria into its rules of legal validity.
- **Exclusive Positivist** — legal validity must remain identifiable through social/source facts rather than moral merits themselves.
- **Normative Positivist** — source-based legality is defended as an institutional and democratic virtue that limits merits-based judicial lawmaking.

Normative Positivism is orthogonal to the Inclusive/Exclusive conceptual dispute. The public modifier engine shows the strongest supported substantive branch rather than forcing every Positivist into a complete partition.

### Non-Positivist subtype umbrella

Substantive descriptive modifiers:

- **Moral-Impact Non-Positivist** → Mark Greenberg.
- **Moral-Practice Non-Positivist** → Scott Hershovitz.
- **Extreme-Injustice Non-Positivist** → Gustav Radbruch / Philip Soper.

These modifiers deliberately describe the jurisprudential theory rather than repeating the champion's surname.

### Natural Lawyer / Interpretivist / Fullerian

No substantive modifier is currently locked. These nouns use the generic fit modifier unless later research reveals a genuine intermediate camp that changes the jurist type.

## Generic fit modifier

When no substantive modifier applies:

- **Unmistakably a [noun]** or **Unmistakably an [vowel-initial noun]** for an unusually clean, well-separated match.
- **[noun]** for the deliberately narrow middle band.
- **Some kind of [noun]** for a weak, mixed, or close nearest-neighbor match.

The indefinite article is generated from the actual rendered noun phrase; it is never omitted from the Unmistakably form.

Current fit gates remain theory-derived rather than population-calibrated: Unmistakably requires identity score >= .82 and margin >= .07; Some kind of applies below .72 or with margin < .04.

## Locked Source champion units

### Positivist
1. **H. L. A. Hart** — baseline social-rule/open-texture positivism.
2. **Hans Kelsen** — Pure Theory: hierarchical legal validity culminating in a presupposed basic norm.
3. **John Austin** — command/sovereign positivism.
4. **Scott Shapiro** — Planning Theory.
5. **Jules Coleman / Matthew Kramer** — generic Inclusive Positivism.

**Wil Waluchow** is split out only when Inclusive Positivism combines with Common-Law Constitutionalism; that cross-axis combination is handled by the non-originalist constitutional-time champion layer rather than making Waluchow the default Inclusive-Positivist endpoint.

6. **Joseph Raz** — Exclusive Positivism.
7. **Tom Campbell** — Normative/Ethical Positivism.

Gardner, Green, Himma, Marmor, and related figures remain further reading unless a separately measured phenotype requires them.

### Interpretivist
8. **Ronald Dworkin** — central legal Interpretivism: institutional practice bears on law through the principles that best fit and justify it.

Nicos Stavropoulos and T.R.S. Allan remain important related scholarship.

### Natural Lawyer
9. **John Finnis** — weak/central-case modern Natural Law: objective moral order plus a substantial settlement, coordination, and institutional-authority role for positive law.
10. **Michael S. Moore** — Strong Natural Law: some serious normative defects can defeat legal validity itself.

Aquinas, Blackstone, Mark Murphy, Robert George, Jonathan Crowe, and related thinkers remain lineage/further reading.

### Fullerian
11. **Lon Fuller** — internal morality/principles of legality.

### Non-Positivist subtype branches
12. **Mark Greenberg** — Moral Impact Theory.
13. **Scott Hershovitz** — moral-practice / one-system nonpositivism.
14. **Gustav Radbruch / Philip Soper** — extreme-injustice threshold.

Robert Alexy remains primarily a Proportionalist champion rather than a duplicate Extreme-Injustice Source champion.

## Adaptive diagnostics

The global adaptive cap remains twelve questions.

- **D-POS1** — Inclusive versus Exclusive Positivism.
- **D-AUS1** — Austinian command/sovereign theory.
- **D-KEL1** — Kelsenian normative hierarchy/basic norm.
- **D-SHA1** — Shapiro Planning Theory.
- **D-POS2** — Normative/Ethical Positivism.
- **D-NP1** — Natural Law: objective morality plus positive-law settlement.
- **D-NP2** — Interpretivism: principles, fit, justification, and discovery rather than judicial creation.
- **D-NP4** — Moral Impact Theory / Greenberg.
- **D-NP5** — Moral-Practice nonpositivism / Hershovitz.
- **D-NL2** — Strong Natural Law / Michael Moore.
- **D-NP6** — Extreme-Injustice threshold / Radbruch-Soper.
- **D-NP3** — Fullerian internal legality.

The router stops speculative searches for competing Source theories once a real noun or narrow branch is affirmatively established. In particular, affirmed Fullerian internal legality ends the fallback search for Natural Law and Interpretivism when the core supplies no clue to either. Independently supported Source theories retain their targeted probes, including when Fullerian evidence is also present. If the respondent is nonpositive on the broad Source axis but rejects the diagnosable nonpositivist theories, Source alone does not manufacture a Non-Positivist noun; another established jurisprudential commitment wins, or the explicit Mixed fallback is used.

Source refinement is eligible when the Source family is live in the identity race or the Source score is extreme (at most 18 or at least 82). It also explores an unresolved Mixed result when the Source score lies in the existing nonpositive diagnostic zone (at most 38). This avoids a circular gate: Natural Law and distinctive nonpositivist theories need affirmative diagnostic evidence before they can enter the public identity race. Neutral Source scores do not unlock that exploration, and an already-established competing family keeps the ordinary candidate-aware gate. A secondary historical Time score never suppresses a live Source family; Originalist source-sensitive champions also retain their targeted Source-evidence refinement.

## Reachability contract

The audit suite requires every locked Source champion unit to be separately reachable from literal 88-item answer vectors through the live adaptive router within the twelve-question cap.

It also requires:

- Positivist to remain the public noun for Inclusive, Exclusive, and Normative branches.
- Interpretivist to be public while Dworkinian remains internal.
- Greenberg and Hershovitz to resolve as descriptive Non-Positivist modifiers rather than eponymous nouns.
- Radbruch/Soper to resolve as Extreme-Injustice Non-Positivist even when the respondent is otherwise comparatively source-positive.
- Fullerian to remain its own public noun.
- Generic fit output to include the correct indefinite article, e.g. **Unmistakably a Positivist** and **Unmistakably an Interpretivist**.
