# LAWvalues Canon Audit — Revised Identity Map

This audit governs the **middle identity** in the result title.

> **[modifier] [best real jurisprudential identity] [phrase]**

The middle identity should be the narrowest real school, theory, or established descriptor that LAWvalues can actually justify. The front modifier and trailing phrase then communicate the respondent's strongest important traits that the middle identity does not already convey.

The audit deliberately separates:
1. **real middle identities**,
2. **champions / exemplars**,
3. **historical lineage**, and
4. **important legal movements outside the quiz's present scope**.

Machine-readable versions live in `result_catalog.js` and `diagnostics.js`. Champion-selection notes live in `CHAMPION_MATRIX.md`.

## The canon filter

A candidate middle identity must pass all of these:

1. **Realness:** an established school, theory, or recognized jurisprudential descriptor.
2. **Distinctiveness:** meaningfully different from neighboring identities.
3. **Diagnosability:** the 93-item core plus at most a few adaptive questions can actually identify it.
4. **Naming fit:** the public-facing label is not something LAWvalues invented.
5. **Coverage value:** it explains a real region of the existing result space.
6. **No-overclaim rule:** when subtype evidence is insufficient, fall back to the broader real label.
7. **Marginal-coverage rule for champions:** a famous figure does not earn a selectable champion slot unless they explain a measured combination that the identity label and existing champion pool do not already explain just as well.

Historical importance by itself is not enough.

# 1. Nature of law

## Core identities

### Positivist
Central, broad, and directly measured.

Adaptive children:
- **Inclusive Positivist**
- **Exclusive Positivist**

Do not infer either subtype merely from textualism or moral conservatism. Use a direct incorporation/source-thesis diagnostic.

### Interpretivist / Dworkinian
Interpretivism is a real jurisprudential family. **Dworkinian** is preferable when the respondent specifically endorses principles as existing law plus fit-and-justification / right-answer reasoning.

### Natural Lawyer
Core identity for classical natural-law reasoning. The key discriminator from Dworkin is not simply "morality matters"; Finnis-style natural law also gives positive law a major settlement/co-ordination function where practical reason underdetermines the answer.

### Radbruchian
A legitimate narrow identity when the extreme-injustice threshold specifically appears.

### Fullerian
Legitimate only after targeted diagnostics on the internal morality / conditions of legality. The current core does not justify the name by itself.

### Alexyan
Legitimate but rare. Require a claim-to-correctness / dual-nature nonpositivism pattern plus structured proportionality. Generic balancing is not enough.

## Conditional contemporary identity

### Common-Good Constitutionalist
This is a real theory, not an invented label, but it is too specific to infer from Natural Law alone. Only use after a targeted common-good/classical-law diagnostic and an otherwise compatible profile.

## Champion logic

- Hart = broad modern positivism / social practice.
- Raz = exclusive/source thesis.
- Dworkin = interpretivism.
- Finnis = modern classical natural law.
- Pojanowski/Walsh = special Natural Law + Originalism + positive-law-settlement combination.
- Fuller = internal legality.
- Radbruch = extreme-injustice threshold.
- Alexy = nonpositivism + claim to correctness + proportionality.
- Blackstone/Aquinas = historical lineage unless a much more specific diagnostic is ever added.

# 2. Realism and pragmatism

The earlier audit underweighted this area.

## Legal Realist
**Direct identity, special diagnostic override.**

Legal Realism is a major American jurisprudential movement and does something the eight axes do not: it questions how determinative legal doctrine really is in hard cases and emphasizes empirical attention to what judges/institutions actually do.

Realism is not the same as Pragmatism.

A person can be:
- descriptively Realist,
- normatively rule-oriented,
- highly Textualist,
- Narrow-remedial,
- and Majoritarian.

Therefore Realism gets one universal soft probe and two follow-ups only if unlocked.

Champions:
- **Karl Llewellyn** = best general mature-American-Realist champion.
- **Brian Leiter** = modern champion for the distinctive Realist + Positivist/Naturalist combination; his reconstruction of Realism and naturalized jurisprudence gives him marginal coverage the historical figures do not.
- **Jerome Frank** = important psychological/fact-skeptical lineage, but not selectable until LAWvalues actually diagnoses that subtype.
- **Oliver Wendell Holmes Jr.** = crucial precursor; potentially informative specifically when Realism combines with strong democratic deference.

## Pragmatist
**Core direct identity.**

Use when forward-looking consequences and workability systematically dominate across domains.

Champion:
- **Richard Posner** = cleanest general champion.
- **Stephen Breyer** = important judicial relative when purposivism + consequences + functionalism are central.

Law & Economics remains outside scope as a separate identity: Posner's pragmatism matters to this quiz even when efficiency theory does not.

# 3. Statutory / legal interpretation

## Textualist
Core identity. Contextual textualism counts; literalism does not define the school.

Champions:
- **John Manning** = cleaner pure-theory champion.
- **Antonin Scalia** = better only when the respondent also matches public-meaning originalism, Rules, and/or structural formalism.
- **Hugo Black** = cross-axis judicial champion when text/language-bound, categorical anti-discretion reasoning combines with unusually strong enforcement of enumerated rights; do not treat total incorporation as measured unless LAWvalues later asks it directly.

## Intentionalist
**Added by this audit.**

This was a real omission. Intentionalism is a leading theory of legal interpretation and must not be silently folded into purposivism.

The adaptive router should distinguish:
- actual enactor/legislative intent
from
- objective statutory purpose.

## Purposivist
Core identity.

Possible champions depend on the rest of the profile:
- Hart & Sacks when Legal Process features are present,
- Breyer when pragmatic consequences/function are stronger,
- Aharon Barak when purposive interpretation combines with proportionality.

## Legal-Process Theorist
Real identity when purposive interpretation combines with institutional competence, role allocation, settlement, and reasoned elaboration.

Champions: **Henry Hart & Albert Sacks**.

## Interpretive Pluralist
Real fallback, not a glamorous headline. Use only when several recognized methods are coherently combined and no one method deserves to win. It is distinct from Chaos/Dummy.

# 4. Constitutional time

Originalism gets several entries because the literature contains several genuinely different theories, not because LAWvalues should favor Originalism.

## Originalist
Core fallback parent.

### Public-Meaning Originalist
Use when public communicative/legal meaning at adoption controls.

Potential champions vary:
- **Solum** for public meaning + fixation + principle/construction sophistication;
- **Scalia** for public meaning + Textualism + Rules/formalism;
- **Barrett** for a more precedent-sensitive version.

### Original-Intent Originalist
Real theory. Do not infer merely from failure to endorse public meaning; require affirmative intent evidence.

### Original-Methods Originalist
Real McGinnis/Rappaport theory.

### Framework Originalist
Real Balkin theory: fixed framework plus substantial legitimate later construction.

## Living Constitutionalist
Core direct identity when constitutional content itself can evolve.

Potential judicial champion: **Justice Brennan** for a strongly rights-protective version. Do not make Brennan the definition of Living Constitutionalism.

## Common-Law Constitutionalist
Core distinctive identity. **David Strauss** is an unusually clean champion because precedent/tradition as constitutional law is the theory itself.

## Principle-oriented vs expected-applications-heavy originalism
Usually better treated as a **front modifier/subtype trait** rather than inventing another middle identity. T8/T9/T11 already carry useful information here.

# 5. Judicial review and constitutional authority

These are conditional identities for respondents whose Posture results are unusually distinctive.

## Thayerian
Clear-error / beyond-reasonable-doubt judicial deference.

Champion: **James Bradley Thayer**.
Historical practitioners/relatives can include Holmes, Brandeis, Hand, Cardozo, and Frankfurter.

## Waldronian
Democratic-legitimacy objection to judicial finality under functioning institutions and reasonable rights disagreement.

Champion: **Jeremy Waldron**.

## Popular Constitutionalist
Not merely "super Majoritarian." It adds rejection of judicial supremacy and independent constitutional authority in the people / democratic politics.

Champion: **Larry Kramer**; **Mark Tushnet** for more radical/weak-form variants.

## Departmentalist
Branches retain independent interpretive authority. Distinct from the broader popular-authority claim.

Do not force a single champion until the diagnostic pattern is clear.

## Elyan
Representation reinforcement: defer ordinarily, intervene when political processes malfunction or exclude groups.

Champion: **John Hart Ely**.

## Bickelian
Passive virtues / strategic avoidance / counter-majoritarian difficulty.

Champion: **Alexander Bickel**.

## Judicial Minimalist
Broader real identity for narrow/incompletely theorized decisions.

Champion: **Cass Sunstein**; Bickel as an important precursor.

# 6. Separation, rules, proportionality, remedies

## Formalist
Core real identity only when structural/legal formalism is genuinely central.

Champions depend on the actual form:
- **Gary Lawson** for hard separation-of-powers formalism.
- **Scalia** when the profile also has text/public-meaning/rules.
- **Frederick Schauer** for rule-formalism as allocation of decisionmaking power.

## Functionalist
Core real separation-of-powers identity.

Champion: **Peter Strauss**.
Breyer can be a judicial relative for a broader pragmatic/functionalist profile.

## Proportionalist
Conditional real label. Require **structured proportionality**, not generic balancing.

Champions:
- **Robert Alexy** for the theoretical principles/proportionality account.
- **Aharon Barak** for judicial application / purposive-proportionality bundle.
- **German Federal Constitutional Court / proportionality tradition** as an institutional champion.
- **Israeli Supreme Court (Barak era)** only with era/doctrine qualification.

## Remedies do not need a fake middle school
Systemic versus narrow remedial instincts usually belong in the modifier or trailing phrase.

Champions:
- **Owen Fiss** = structural reform / institutional restructuring.
- **Abram Chayes** = public-law litigation / managerial judge.
- **Samuel Bray** = plaintiff-specific / traditional-equity limits.

Do not invent "Structuralist," "Remedialist," or similar pseudo-schools.

If a respondent's only highly distinctive feature is Remedies, the title can use a neutral broader real identity in the middle and let the modifier/phrase carry the remedial information; champion display can still be Fiss/Chayes/Bray.

# 7. Historical canon and why it does not automatically become a result

## Blackstone
Canonical and useful, but not currently a clean modern identity. His jurisprudence bundles natural law, common-law tradition, positive institutional settlement, and eighteenth-century constitutional assumptions. Keep as historical lineage / occasional champion.

## Aquinas
Foundational natural-law lineage.

## Austin / Bentham
Foundational modern positivist/command-theory lineage.

## Kelsen
Major modern positivist architect, but LAWvalues does not test the Pure Theory / basic-norm architecture closely enough for "Kelsenian."

## Holmes
Canonical precursor to Realism and a major restraint figure, but mature Realism is better represented by Llewellyn/Frank when the Realist identity is actually confirmed.

Historical stature is not a classification criterion.

# 8. Outside scope, deliberately

Important but not presently worth separate diagnostic trees:

- Critical Legal Studies
- Law & Economics
- feminist jurisprudence
- Critical Race Theory
- republican / anti-domination constitutionalism

Reason: these require substantive theoretical questions that are not doing much work in the present eight-axis instrument. LAWvalues should not collect schools merely because they are famous.

# 9. Adaptive diagnostic priorities

## High priority
1. Realism universal soft probe + two follow-ups.
2. Originalist subtype routing: public meaning vs intent; Original Methods; construction/framework if needed.
3. Positivist routing: inclusive vs exclusive.
4. Nonpositivist routing: Natural Lawyer vs Dworkinian; Fuller/Radbruch only if unresolved.
5. Extreme-Majoritarian routing: anti-judicial supremacy -> Popular Constitutionalist vs Departmentalist; Ely if conditional-process pattern appears.
6. Intentionalist vs Purposivist when open-construction answers are ambiguous.

## Medium priority
7. Legal Process vs generic Purposivism/Pragmatism.
8. Bickelian vs generic Minimalism if core O7 is insufficient.
9. Proportionalist only if structured proportionality would materially improve on a balancing modifier.
10. Common-Good Constitutionalist only in the rare profile where it is genuinely plausible.

## Usually no extra diagnostics needed
- Common-Law Constitutionalism
- generic Textualism
- generic Purposivism
- hard Separation Formalism/Functionalism
- strong Rules orientation
- systemic vs narrow Remedies
- correctness-first precedent posture

# 10. Champion selection

There is no "Originalist champion" or "Textualist champion" by fiat.

After the middle identity is chosen, compare only compatible champions using:
- matched signature traits,
- contradicted signature traits,
- the respondent's trait importance,
- available adaptive diagnostics.

Examples:

**Public-Meaning Originalist**
- Solum if principle/construction nuance dominates.
- Scalia if Textualism + Rules + structural Formalism dominate.
- Barrett if Stability/settlement is unusually strong.
- Thomas if correctness-first precedent skepticism dominates.

**Legal Realist**
- Llewellyn by default for broad law-in-action / rule-skeptical Realism.
- Frank if later diagnostics show strong psychological/fact skepticism.
- Holmes as lineage/precursor rather than automatic winner.

**Purposivist**
- Hart & Sacks if institutional competence / Legal Process.
- Breyer if consequences/pragmatism/function.
- Barak if proportionality + purposive rights interpretation.

**Natural Lawyer**
- Finnis for modern classical natural law.
- Pojanowski/Walsh for Natural Law + Originalism + positive-law settlement.
- Blackstone/Aquinas as lineage rather than automatic closest champion.

If the top champion is not materially better than the next one, do not force a single person. Show multiple intellectual relatives or just the identity.


# 11. Champion-pool pruning

The champion pool should be **sparse**.

A candidate is not added because:
- the person is famous,
- the person appears in a survey,
- the person influenced another champion,
- or the person is a good example of an already-named school.

A candidate is added when their whole jurisprudential signature gives LAWvalues **marginal explanatory coverage**.

Current implications:

- Thayer makes generic Frankfurter/Hand/Brandeis restraint largely redundant.
- Scalia remains useful because his combination of textualism, public-meaning originalism, Rules, and structural formalism is distinctive and directly measurable.
- Hugo Black adds a different measurable combination: text/rule absolutism plus strong enumerated-rights enforcement.
- Llewellyn remains the general mature-Realist champion.
- Brian Leiter adds a modern Realist + Positivist/Naturalist combination and therefore fills a different region.
- Frank remains lineage until fact/psychology diagnostics exist.
- Posner remains because Pragmatism itself is a direct identity; Law & Economics is irrelevant to why he is included.
- Bork should be considered only if Original-Intent Originalism is affirmatively diagnosed; he is not needed as another generic Originalist.
- Easterbrook is presently redundant once Law & Economics is excluded.

This rule should be applied to every future suggestion, including names supplied by the user or the model.
