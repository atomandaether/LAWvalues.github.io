This audit governs the **middle identity** in the result title.

> **[modifier] [best real jurisprudential identity] [phrase]**

The middle identity should be the narrowest real school, theory, or established descriptor that LAWvalues can actually justify. The front modifier and trailing phrase then communicate the respondent's strongest important traits that the middle identity does not already convey.

The audit deliberately separates:
1. **real middle identities**,
2. **champions / exemplars**,
3. **historical lineage**, and
4. **important legal movements outside the quiz's present scope**.

Machine-readable versions live in `result_catalog.js` and `diagnostics.js`. Champion-selection notes live in `CHAMPION_MATRIX.md`.

## The canon filter

A candidate middle identity must pass all of these:

1. **Realness:** an established school, theory, or recognized jurisprudential descriptor.
2. **Distinctiveness:** meaningfully different from neighboring identities.
3. **Diagnosability:** the 88-item core plus at most a few adaptive questions can actually identify it.
4. **Naming fit:** the public-facing label is not something LAWvalues invented.
5. **Coverage value:** it explains a real region of the existing result space.
6. **No-overclaim rule:** when subtype evidence is insufficient, fall back to the broader real label.
7. **Marginal-coverage rule for champions:** a famous figure does not earn a selectable champion slot unless they explain a measured combination that the identity label and existing champion pool do not already explain just as well.

Historical importance by itself is not enough.

# 1. Nature of law

## Core identities

### Positivist
Central, broad, and directly measured.

Adaptive children:
- **Inclusive Positivist**
- **Exclusive Positivist**

Do not infer either subtype merely from textualism or moral conservatism. Use a direct incorporation/source-thesis diagnostic.

### Interpretivist / Dworkinian
Interpretivism is a real jurisprudential family. **Dworkinian** is preferable when the respondent specifically endorses principles as existing law plus fit-and-justification / right-answer reasoning.

### Natural Lawyer
Core identity for classical natural-law reasoning. The key discriminator from Dworkin is not simply "morality matters"; Finnis-style natural law also gives positive law a major settlement/co-ordination function where practical reason underdetermines the answer.

### Radbruchian
A legitimate narrow identity when the extreme-injustice threshold specifically appears.

### Fullerian
Legitimate only after targeted diagnostics on the internal morality / conditions of legality. The current core does not justify the name by itself.

### Alexy-shaped proportionality
Do **not** use Alexyan as a public noun. The public noun is **Proportionalist** when structured proportionality is directly diagnosed. A nonpositivist/correctness-heavy version is a modifier-level subtype, and **Robert Alexy** remains the champion-level match. This prevents a person-derived noun from duplicating the champion layer.

## Conditional contemporary identity

### Common-Good Constitutionalist
This is a real theory, not an invented label, but it is too specific to infer from Natural Law alone. Only use after a targeted common-good/classical-law diagnostic and an otherwise compatible profile.

## Champion logic

- Hart = broad modern positivism / social practice.
- Raz = exclusive/source thesis.
- Dworkin = interpretivism.
- Finnis = modern classical natural law.
- Pojanowski/Walsh = special Natural Law + Originalism + positive-law-settlement combination.
- Fuller = internal legality.
- Radbruch = extreme-injustice threshold.
- Alexy = champion for Proportionalism when nonpositivist/correctness features dominate.
- Blackstone/Aquinas = historical lineage unless a much more specific diagnostic is ever added.

# 2. Realism and pragmatism

The earlier audit underweighted this area.

## Legal Realist
**Direct identity, special diagnostic override.**

Legal Realism is a major American jurisprudential movement and does something the eight axes do not: it questions how determinative legal doctrine really is in hard cases and emphasizes empirical attention to what judges/institutions actually do.

Realism is not the same as Pragmatism.

A person can be:
- descriptively Realist,
- normatively rule-oriented,
- highly Textualist,
- Narrow-remedial,
- and Majoritarian.

Therefore Realism gets one universal soft probe and two follow-ups only if unlocked.

Champions:
- **Karl Llewellyn** = best general mature-American-Realist champion.
- **Brian Leiter** = modern champion for the distinctive Realist + Positivist/Naturalist combination; his reconstruction of Realism and naturalized jurisprudence gives him marginal coverage the historical figures do not.
- **Jerome Frank** = important psychological/fact-skeptical lineage, but not selectable until LAWvalues actually diagnoses that subtype.
- **Oliver Wendell Holmes Jr.** = crucial precursor; potentially informative specifically when Realism combines with strong democratic deference.

## Pragmatist
**Core direct identity.**

Use when forward-looking consequences and workability systematically dominate across domains.

Champion:
- **Richard Posner** = cleanest general champion.
- **Stephen Breyer** = important judicial relative when purposivism + consequences + functionalism are central.

Law & Economics remains outside scope as a separate identity: Posner's pragmatism matters to this quiz even when efficiency theory does not.

# 3. Statutory / legal interpretation

## Textualist
Core identity. Contextual textualism counts; literalism does not define the school.

Champions:
- **John Manning** = cleaner pure-theory champion.
- **Antonin Scalia** = better only when the respondent also matches public-meaning originalism, Rules, and/or structural formalism.
- **Hugo Black** = cross-axis judicial champion when text/language-bound, categorical anti-discretion reasoning combines with unusually strong enforcement of enumerated rights; do not treat total incorporation as measured unless LAWvalues later asks it directly.

## Intentionalist
**Added by this audit.**

This was a real omission. Intentionalism is a leading theory of legal interpretation and must not be silently folded into purposivism.

The adaptive router should distinguish:
- actual enactor/legislative intent
from
- objective statutory purpose.

## Purposivist
Core identity.

Possible champions depend on the rest of the profile:
- Hart & Sacks when Legal Process features are present,
- Breyer when pragmatic consequences/function are stronger,
- Aharon Barak when purposive interpretation combines with proportionality.

## Legal-Process Theorist
Real identity when purposive interpretation combines with institutional competence, role allocation, settlement, and reasoned elaboration.

Champions: **Henry Hart & Albert Sacks**.

## Interpretive Pluralist
Real fallback, not a glamorous headline. Use only when several recognized methods are coherently combined and no one method deserves to win. It is distinct from Chaos/Dummy.

# 4. Constitutional time

Originalism gets several entries because the literature contains several genuinely different theories, not because LAWvalues should favor Originalism.

## Originalist
Core fallback parent.

### Public-Meaning Originalist
Use when public communicative/legal meaning at adoption controls.

Potential champions vary:
- **Solum** for public meaning + fixation + principle/construction sophistication;
- **Scalia** for public meaning + Textualism + Rules/formalism;
- **Barrett** for a more precedent-sensitive version.

### Original-Intent Originalist
Real theory. Do not infer merely from failure to endorse public meaning; require affirmative intent evidence.

### Original-Methods Originalist
Real McGinnis/Rappaport theory.

### Framework Originalist
Real Balkin theory: fixed framework plus substantial legitimate later construction.

## Living Constitutionalist
Core direct identity when constitutional content itself can evolve.

Locked champion structure:
- **William J. Brennan Jr.** — default strong contemporary-ratification / rights-protective Living champion.
- **Robert Post / Reva Siegel** — **Democratic Living Constitutionalist** modifier: sustained public constitutional contestation and political mobilization participate in constitutional development while judicially enforceable rights remain part of the system.
- **Karl Klare / Pius Langa** — **Transformative Living Constitutionalist** modifier: constitutional interpretation and enforcement operate as a continuing project of institutional and social transformation.

Democratic and Transformative are modifiers, not additional nouns. Dworkin/Fleming remain principally Source/Interpretivist; Bobbitt/Fallon principally Method; Kramer/Tushnet/Gardbaum principally Posture.

## Common-Law Constitutionalist
Core distinctive identity. **David Strauss** remains the default clean champion because precedent/tradition as constitutional law is the theory itself.

Two diagnostic specialists clear the Alexander-v-Bork threshold:
- **John Marshall Harlan II** when common-law development is specifically framed as historically embedded ordered liberty / living tradition and paired with substantial restraint.
- **Lord Cooke of Thorndon** when fundamental common-law rights are treated as possible limits on ordinary legislative supremacy.

Do not infer either specialist from generic Living Constitutionalism or respect for precedent alone.

**Wil Waluchow** is a separate cross-axis Common-Law champion only when the respondent combines Inclusive Positivism with common-law constitutional development. Generic Inclusive Positivism remains Coleman/Kramer; generic Common-Law Constitutionalism remains Strauss.

## Principle-oriented vs expected-applications-heavy originalism
Usually better treated as a **front modifier/subtype trait** rather than inventing another middle identity. T8/T9/T11 already carry useful information here.

# 5. Judicial review and constitutional authority

The Posture zone uses six public nouns. Broad strong-form legal constitutionalism and weak-form review remain measured authority configurations but are no longer public nouns.

## Political Constitutionalist
Functioning representative institutions retain the stronger claim to final constitutional authority under reasonable rights disagreement.

Champion unit: **Jeremy Waldron / Richard Bellamy**. Their philosophical rationales differ, but the present instrument does not measure enough to separate them without violating the Alexander/Kaye threshold. Griffith, Tomkins, and Webber remain related reading.

**Waldronian** is retained only as an internal legacy alias.

## Thayerian
Courts retain judicial review but invalidate legislation only when the legislature's constitutional judgment falls outside the range of reasonable interpretation.

Champion: **James Bradley Thayer**. Holmes, Hand, Brandeis, Cardozo, and Frankfurter remain historical practitioners/relatives.

## Popular Constitutionalist
Ultimate constitutional authority remains with the people and democratic constitutional politics rather than becoming the exclusive property of courts.

Champion: **Larry Kramer**. Tushnet remains important related reading but is not a separate locked endpoint.

## Departmentalist
Coordinate governmental branches retain independent good-faith constitutional interpretive authority within their own functions.

Champion: **Kevin Walsh** for judicial departmentalism. Lincoln and Jefferson remain historical lineage. Departmentalism is narrower than Popular Constitutionalism because it need not make ordinary popular constitutional politics the ultimate source of interpretive authority.

## Political Process Jurist
Ordinary substantive disagreements remain with democratic politics, while courts intervene more aggressively when democratic channels are blocked, distorted, or exclusionary.

Default champion: **John Hart Ely**.

**Responsive Political Process Jurist** is the one substantive modifier in this noun. It routes to **Rosalind Dixon**, whose responsive judicial review calibrates timing, intensity, reasoning, and remedies to the specific democratic dysfunction and to judicial capacity.

**Elyan** is retained only as an internal legacy alias.

## Judicial Minimalist
Narrow, shallow, incompletely theorized adjudication that resolves no more than necessary.

Default champion: **Cass Sunstein**.

**Passive-virtues Judicial Minimalist** is the substantive modifier and routes to **Alexander Bickel**. Passive virtues mean strategic avoidance, timing, and justiciability as responses to the counter-majoritarian difficulty, not merely narrow remedies.

## Routing
- O1 plus rejection of the Waldron/Bellamy democratic-finality rationale supports Thayer.
- D-WAL1 separates Thayerian deference from Political Constitutionalism.
- O6 still supplies weak-form/dialogic institutional-design evidence, but no public noun.
- D-MAJ1 must be followed by D-MAJ2 when agreed: D-MAJ2 separates Popular Constitutionalism from branch-specific Departmentalism.
- D-ELY1 establishes Political Process Jurisprudence; **D-RESP1** is the only new diagnostic and distinguishes Dixon from Ely.
- O7 supplies passive-virtues evidence; R6/R7/R11 and the Remedies axis supply generic narrow/shallow Minimalism.
- Strong rights-protective Posture answers can independently reach Legal Constitutionalism without requiring a bespoke champion.

# 6. Institutional structure, doctrinal form, and remedies

## Formalist
A single public noun covers two ways formal constraint can itself become the respondent's primary jurisprudential commitment:

- **structural Formalism** — categorical constitutional assignment of governmental powers and prescribed institutional procedures;
- **rule Formalism** — rules deliberately constrain decision-makers even when an all-things-considered judgment would produce a better result.

Locked champions:
- **Gary Lawson** — default hard structural Formalism / nondelegation. His champion vector no longer borrows Originalist Time merely because Lawson is also an Originalist.
- **Frederick Schauer** — rule Formalism / anti-discretion. A sufficiently dominant Rules profile can now reach Formalist directly rather than falling through to Interpretive Pluralism.
- **Steven Calabresi / Saikrishna Prakash** — unitary-executive Formalism when E2 affirmatively supplies the presidential-control proposition.

Scalia remains cross-cutting only when Textualism/Originalism is the stronger overall fixation. Kathleen Sullivan is important rules-versus-standards lineage rather than another champion.

## Functionalist
Core separation-of-powers identity centered on workability, accountability, tolerated institutional overlap, and preservation of each branch's core capacities.

Locked champion:
- **Peter Strauss** — default Functionalist.
Breyer and Justice White remain related judicial exemplars rather than separate endpoints.

## Proportionalist
Structured proportionality remains a public noun only after affirmative **D-PROP1** evidence. Generic balancing is not enough.

Locked champions:
- **Robert Alexy** — nonpositivist/principles-heavy Proportionalism.
- **Aharon Barak** — purposive, rights-protective judicial Proportionalism.

The existing public modifiers remain:
- **Higher-law Proportionalist** for the Alexy-shaped nonpositivist version.
- **Purpose-led Proportionalist** for the Barak-shaped purposive version.

D-PROP1 is now routed early only for genuinely plausible proportionality profiles so it can beat generic Interpretivism without wasting the diagnostic budget on ordinary pragmatists or purposivists.

Möller and Beatty remain lineage: their distinctive theories require propositions the current instrument does not directly measure.

## Remedies do not become a noun
Remedies remain orthogonal to the respondent's main jurisprudential identity. A user still receives one noun and exactly one champion unit.

### Selectable remedial champions

**P. N. Bhagwati / V. R. Krishna Iyer** remain selectable because their social-action/PIL package crosses Remedies, Method, Posture, access to court, and judicial role. The existing D-PIL1 gate remains required.

**Owen Fiss** remains the one exceptional cross-noun remedial champion. Fiss may enter the single global champion competition only when:
- systemic Remedies are extremely salient;
- structural reform and continuing judicial supervision are both strongly supported;
- the judicial-role profile is strongly counter-majoritarian; and
- either no strong noun-compatible champion exists or Remedies are materially more distinctive than every other axis.

Thus a Barak-like respondent with broad remedies remains Barak. Fiss wins only when structural-reform adjudication actually explains the respondent better as a whole.

### Lineage / explanation only

Champion-level remedies specialists now include **Samuel Bray** (traditional equity), **Mila Sohoni / Amanda Frost** (universal relief), **Henry Monaghan** (constitutional common law), and **Daryl Levinson** (remedial equilibration). Each requires affirmative dedicated diagnostic evidence and none creates a Remedies noun.

**Abram Chayes** remains nonselectable lineage closely overlapping Fiss.

## Single champion rule

Every valid classified result receives **exactly one champion unit**.

Noun compatibility remains a substantial advantage because the champion should normally explain the user's primary jurisprudential identity. Specific affirmative evidence breaks ties in favor of a narrower genuinely measured unit. Fiss is the only current cross-noun exception and is subject to the high whole-profile gate above.

Close races lower champion confidence; they never produce a second champion. Response-quality failures and withheld/incoherent results remain the obvious exception.

# 7. Historical canon and why it does not automatically become a result

## Blackstone
Canonical and useful, but not currently a clean modern identity. His jurisprudence bundles natural law, common-law tradition, positive institutional settlement, and eighteenth-century constitutional assumptions. Keep as historical lineage / occasional champion.

## Aquinas
Foundational natural-law lineage.

## Austin / Bentham
Austin is now a selectable command-theory Positivist specialist behind **D-AUS1**. Bentham remains foundational lineage.

## Kelsen
Kelsen is now a selectable Pure-Theory Positivist specialist behind **D-KEL1**; the basic-norm architecture is directly tested rather than inferred from generic source-positivism.

## Holmes
Canonical precursor to Realism and a major restraint figure, but mature Realism is better represented by Llewellyn/Frank when the Realist identity is actually confirmed.

Historical stature is not a classification criterion.

# 9. Adaptive diagnostic priorities

## High priority
1. Realism universal soft probe + two follow-ups.
2. Originalist subtype routing: public meaning vs intent; Original Methods; construction/framework if needed.
3. Positivist routing: inclusive vs exclusive.
4. Nonpositivist routing: Natural Lawyer vs Dworkinian; Fuller/Radbruch only if unresolved.
5. Extreme-Majoritarian routing: anti-judicial supremacy -> Popular Constitutionalist vs Departmentalist; Ely if conditional-process pattern appears.
6. Intentionalist vs Purposivist when open-construction answers are ambiguous.

## Medium priority
7. Legal Process vs generic Purposivism/Pragmatism.
8. Passive-virtues/Bickel-style Minimalism only when it would materially refine the broader Minimalist noun.
9. Proportionalist only if structured proportionality would materially improve on generic balancing; Alexy-vs-Barak distinctions belong in modifiers/champions.
10. Common-Good Constitutionalist only in the rare profile where it is genuinely plausible.

## Usually no extra diagnostics needed
- Common-Law Constitutionalism
- generic Textualism
- generic Purposivism
- hard Separation Formalism/Functionalism
- strong Rules orientation
- systemic vs narrow Remedies
- correctness-first precedent posture

# 10. Champion selection

There is no "Originalist champion" or "Textualist champion" by fiat.

After the middle identity is chosen, compare only compatible champions using:
- matched signature traits,
- contradicted signature traits,
- the respondent's trait importance,
- available adaptive diagnostics.

Examples:

**Public-Meaning Originalist**
- Solum if principle/construction nuance dominates.
- Scalia if Textualism + Rules + structural Formalism dominate.
- Barrett if Stability/settlement is unusually strong.
- Thomas if correctness-first precedent skepticism dominates.

**Legal Realist**
- Llewellyn by default for broad law-in-action / rule-skeptical Realism.
- Frank if later diagnostics show strong psychological/fact skepticism.
- Holmes as lineage/precursor rather than automatic winner.

**Purposivist**
- Hart & Sacks if institutional competence / Legal Process.
- Breyer if consequences/pragmatism/function.
- Barak if proportionality + purposive rights interpretation.

**Natural Lawyer**
- Finnis for modern classical natural law.
- Pojanowski/Walsh for Natural Law + Originalism + positive-law settlement.
- Blackstone/Aquinas as lineage rather than automatic closest champion.

If the top champion is only narrowly ahead of the runner-up, lower the confidence label but still return the single best champion unit. Valid results never display multiple champion units.


# 11. Champion-pool pruning

The champion pool should be **sparse**.

A candidate is not added because:
- the person is famous,
- the person appears in a survey,
- the person influenced another champion,
- or the person is a good example of an already-named school.

A candidate is added when their whole jurisprudential signature gives LAWvalues **marginal explanatory coverage**.

Current implications:

- Thayer makes generic Frankfurter/Hand/Brandeis restraint largely redundant.
- Scalia remains useful because his combination of textualism, public-meaning originalism, Rules, and structural formalism is distinctive and directly measurable.
- Hugo Black adds a different measurable combination: text/rule absolutism plus strong enumerated-rights enforcement.
- Llewellyn remains the general mature-Realist champion.
- Brian Leiter adds a modern Realist + Positivist/Naturalist combination and therefore fills a different region.
- Frank remains lineage until fact/psychology diagnostics exist.
- Posner remains because Pragmatism itself is a direct identity; Law & Economics is irrelevant to why he is included.
- Bork should be considered only if Original-Intent Originalism is affirmatively diagnosed; he is not needed as another generic Originalist.
- Easterbrook is presently redundant once Law & Economics is excluded.

This rule should be applied to every future suggestion, including names supplied by the user or the model.
