The eight public axes remain the instrument. Jurisprudential identities and thinker matches are an interpretive layer built on top of the 88 core responses plus, when useful, no more than twelve adaptive tie-breakers.

## Sparse signatures, not eight-number caricatures

### Noun-conditioned champion compatibility

The public noun is resolved from the respondent's **primary jurisprudential commitment**. Agreement with a subtype does not by itself establish the importance of its parent family. Conditional priority comparisons supply separate evidence when multiple supported families compete. Champion matching uses the whole profile: the winning noun has a compatibility advantage, while independently supported secondary commitments can preserve other compatible thinkers without inheriting that full advantage.

Profiles may declare:

- **primary compatibility** in `identityIds`: the thinker directly exemplifies that noun. This receives the full champion compatibility bonus.
- **cross-cutting compatibility** in `crossCuttingIdentityIds`: the thinker can honestly sit beneath that noun only when an explicit `crossCuttingRequirements` bridge is affirmatively evidenced. Merely listing the noun is not enough. This receives a smaller compatibility bonus.
- a thinker with no compatibility through the primary or independently supported secondary commitments is excluded. Axis proximity cannot rescue an intellectually misleading pairing. Secondary compatibility does not carry the winning noun's full canonicality bonus. When comparative answers establish primary importance, an incompatible secondary-family match cannot override it; a cross-cutting match needs actual evidence covering the primary domain.

Public child identities inherit compatibility from their public parent where appropriate. Thus a champion eligible for generic Originalism can remain eligible beneath Public-Meaning or Original-Intent Originalism, while an unrelated thinker cannot leak into those results.

Examples:
- Hugo Black is primarily **Textualist**, cross-cutting-compatible with **Formalist**, and incompatible with **Public-Meaning Originalist**.
- Scalia is primarily an **Originalist** champion; Manning separately represents mainline **Textualism** rather than being welded into the same default unit.
- Stephen Breyer is primarily **Purposivist** while remaining cross-cutting-compatible with **Pragmatist** and **Functionalist**.
- Benjamin Cardozo is primarily **Pragmatist** while remaining cross-cutting-compatible with **Common-Law Constitutionalist**.

This keeps the causal direction explicit: **respondent → strongest jurisprudential claim → compatible noun → best whole-profile champion**. The system never chooses a noun merely to reach a preferred champion.

A thinker is not assigned a position on every axis. Each selectable thinker has:

- **modeled axes**: only the dimensions that materially characterize the thinker;
- **weights**: how diagnostic those modeled dimensions are;
- **signature traits**: question-level ideas that make the thinker distinctive;
- **required traits / gates**: evidence that must exist before a specialist can win;
- **anti-traits**: strong contrary evidence that should suppress the match;
- **tier**: main, specialist, catch-all, or lineage.

An absent axis is unknown or non-diagnostic, never an implicit 50 or evidence of agreement. Strong commitments outside the available thinker model reduce coverage and confidence without inventing disagreement. Exported match evidence distinguishes modeled/supported axes, unknown axes, known axis disagreements, supported signature traits, and opposing traits. A zero-axis profile establishes a specific theoretical affinity; it cannot support a high-confidence broad match merely because its narrow traits agree.

Matching therefore uses the *shape* of a respondent's profile. Two people can sit on the same side of every axis but differ because one respondent's strongest feature is Textualism while another's is Positivism. A specialist can beat a broad profile when the respondent has the specialist's distinctive spike.

## Three substantive levels

### 1. Major jurisprudential families

A major family remains available even if most of its distinction is concentrated on one axis. Do not prune a real jurisprudential tradition merely because another thinker occupies similar eight-axis space.

Current protected families include:

- Legal Positivism
- Natural Law
- Dworkinian jurisprudence (with Interpretivism retained only as an internal parent where useful)
- Greenbergian Moral Impact Theory / one-system nonpositivism
- Legal Realism
- Pragmatism
- Textualism / Intentionalism / Purposivism
- Legal Process
- Originalism and its genuinely different subtypes
- Living Constitutionalism
- Common-Law Constitutionalism
- Common-Good Constitutionalism when directly diagnosed
- Political Constitutionalism, Thayerian deference, Popular Constitutionalism, Departmentalism, Political Process Jurisprudence, and Judicial Minimalism; strong-form Legal Constitutionalism and Weak-Form review remain internal authority descriptors rather than public nouns
- Structural constitutional interpretation
- Formalism, including separation-of-powers formalism and genuinely dominant rule-formalism
- Functionalist separation of powers
- Structured Proportionality

Natural Law is specifically protected from collapsing into generic Non-Positivism. Finnis is a main modern champion; Fuller and Radbruch require their narrower distinctive evidence. Dworkin likewise is not simply "the non-positivist."

### 2. Diagnosable specialists

A specialist deserves a selectable personal result when a narrow, judicially meaningful idea can distinguish that person from nearby broad results.

Examples:

- Lawrence Solum: public meaning / principle application / construction-zone nuance
- Jack Balkin: fixed framework plus legitimate later construction
- Frederick Schauer: dominant rule-formalism / anti-discretion rather than merely a Rules side-note
- Steven Calabresi / Saikrishna Prakash: unitary-executive Formalism when E2 directly supplies the presidential-control proposition
- James Bradley Thayer: clear-error judicial deference
- Robert Alexy: champion-level fit for Proportionalism when non-positivist correctness claims accompany structured proportionality
- Owen Fiss: exceptional cross-noun champion only when extreme structural remedies, continuing supervision, and strong judicial role form the respondent's most explanatory whole-profile package
- Roscoe Pound: sociological jurisprudence—law in action, competing social interests, and practical social operation
- Henry Hart & Albert Sacks: Legal Process—purpose plus institutional competence, settlement, and reasoned elaboration
- François Gény: Free Scientific Research only after Sociological Jurisprudence is already plausible
- John Marshall Harlan II: living tradition / ordered liberty only inside an already plausible common-law constitutional profile
- Lord Cooke: strong common-law constitutional rights only after the dedicated legislative-supremacy diagnostic
- P. N. Bhagwati / V. R. Krishna Iyer: social-action/PIL judging only when systemic remedies, counter-majoritarian posture, and procedural-access innovation align
- William J. Brennan Jr.: default strong Living champion from contemporary meaning plus rights-protective posture, without needing a bespoke diagnostic
- Robert Post / Reva Siegel: Democratic Living modifier/champion only after a public-contestation constitutional-development diagnostic
- Karl Klare / Pius Langa: Transformative Living modifier/champion only after a continuing constitutional-transformation diagnostic
- Wil Waluchow: Common-Law champion only when Inclusive Positivism and common-law constitutional development are both independently established
- Stephen Gardbaum: retained as weak-form/New Commonwealth lineage; dialogic-review evidence no longer creates a public noun or champion endpoint
- Kevin Walsh: Departmentalism only after branch-specific independent-interpretation evidence
- Rosalind Dixon: Responsive Political Process Jurisprudence only after representation reinforcement is established and the responsive-review diagnostic is affirmed
- Alexander Bickel: Passive-virtues Judicial Minimalism only when strategic avoidance is directly evidenced; generic narrow/shallow Minimalism remains Sunsteinian

A specialist never wins from coordinate proximity alone.

### 3. Lineage and further reading

Important figures can appear on result pages without being eligible to win.

Savigny and the Historical School are the model case. The tradition is major jurisprudential history, but LAWvalues' Historical/Living axis concerns fixation of constitutional meaning, not Savigny's organic development through custom and legal culture. Savigny is therefore further reading from compatible tradition/common-law results rather than a scored endpoint.

This tier also accommodates academics whose work is illuminating but too narrow, insufficiently diagnosable, or redundant with a stronger selectable personality.

Current remedies specialists include Bray, Sohoni/Frost, Monaghan, and Levinson behind dedicated affirmative gates; Abram Chayes remains lineage. Remedies still never create a public noun or a second champion slot.

## Catch-all without false theory

Sandra Day O'Connor is the designated champion for the internal **Mixed Jurisprudence** fallback: a respondent for whom no affirmative public school is established can receive an honest low-theory/case-by-case analogue rather than being forced into a false theory.

Philip Bobbitt covers a different case entirely: **affirmative** methodological pluralism. Interpretive Pluralist now requires actual modal-pluralism evidence; it is never the "none of the above" noun.

## Single champion cardinality

Every valid classified result returns exactly one champion unit. Noun-compatible profiles receive the normal compatibility advantage. Close scores lower confidence rather than creating co-champions. Fiss, Bray, Sohoni/Frost, and Levinson have tightly gated cross-noun remedial routes; other alternatives require independently supported compatible commitments. Narrow agreement is not treated as complete profile coverage.

There is **no global incompatible champion fallback**. If a public noun cannot produce a compatible champion, that is an architecture error for the audit suite to catch, not permission to return the least-wrong thinker from another school. The only no-school path is the explicit internal Mixed Jurisprudence fallback with its designated O'Connor catch-all.

## Adaptive diagnostics

The core always determines the eight displayed scores. Adaptive questions only preserve distinctions compressed by those eight aggregates.

A response check precedes substantive refinement and never affects jurisprudential scores. Legal Realism remains available outside the normative axes, but its probes compete for limited capacity rather than automatically occupying an extended opening chain. Later questions are routed only when they can discriminate live candidates. Extremely fast completion, straight-line responding, excessive skipping, or a failed response check withholds the thinker/result classification rather than turning low-information clicking into a centrist or low-theory match. Examples include:

- Public Meaning vs Original Intent vs Original Methods vs Framework Originalism
- Inclusive vs Exclusive Positivism
- Natural Law vs Dworkinian Interpretivism vs Moral Impact Theory vs Fuller/Radbruch edge cases
- Thayerian deference vs Political Constitutionalism
- weak-form/dialogic review as an institutional descriptor versus genuinely Popular Constitutionalism
- Popular Constitutionalism vs Departmentalism
- Political Process Jurisprudence vs generic deference
- Sunsteinian Minimalism vs Bickelian passive virtues
- Intentionalism vs Purposivism
- Legal Process vs generic Purposivism/Pragmatism
- Structuralism vs text-, history-, purpose-, and structure-formalism explanations
- Pound-style sociological jurisprudence
- structured Proportionality
- Bobbitt-style pluralism vs O'Connor-style low theory

The router stops when no remaining diagnostic is materially useful or after twelve adaptive questions.

## Public result grammar

After identity selection, the public result is assembled as **[noun-conditioned twist] [real jurisprudential identity] [broad residual phrase]**.

The three slots now do different jobs:

1. **Noun:** the respondent's best-supported primary defining commitment. It is not intended to encompass the whole worldview.
2. **Phrase:** the strongest broad characteristic not already communicated by the noun and its selected subtype. Phrase eligibility is deliberately conservative: the public pool consists of the eight axis poles plus `objective_morality` and `anti_discretion`, the two narrower traits that the existing core/diagnostic bank can support with genuine breadth. A one-off answer cannot become a substantive phrase. If no residual direction clears that threshold, the phrase layer distinguishes strong opposing answers that cancel (`who is a walking contradiction`) from genuinely weak/neutral residual answers (`who needs to stop fence sitting`). Conflict on an axis already spent by the noun or selected modifier does not count as the secondary phrase.
3. **Modifier / twist:** a conditional refinement of the noun, not the third-highest global trait. It asks: *given that this person is a [noun], what is unusually informative about the particular kind of [noun] they are?* Modifiers use narrower subtype evidence when it establishes a meaningful distinction within the selected noun, or existing strength-of-fit wording when no such subtype is established. They do not introduce an unrelated secondary commitment.

The modifier layer is deliberately **between noun and champion** in granularity. It should capture a real sub-umbrella or meaningful twist shared by multiple plausible champions, not merely rename the runner-up noun and not reproduce an individual scholar's signature doctrine. If no measured subtype genuinely improves on the noun, the result uses an explicit archetypal/default modifier rather than inventing fake precision. Alexy-shaped proportionality and Bickel-shaped minimalism are model cases: **Proportionalist** and **Judicial Minimalist** stay the nouns; the finer distinction moves to modifier/champion level.

This distinction matters. A Solum-like respondent may be a **Public-Meaning Originalist** whose strongest broad residual characteristic is textual constraint, while the front modifier captures the more specific fixed-meaning / construction-zone package that makes that originalism Solum-like. The title can therefore say something like **Elbow-room Public-Meaning Originalist ...** rather than spending both public joke slots on the respondent's second- and third-largest axis bars.

Phrase selection still spends noun redundancy. Twist selection is noun-conditioned and also suppresses a twist when the trailing phrase already communicates the same concept. High-value cross-axis combinations may additionally produce an explanatory bridge paragraph. Closest-thinker matching remains a separate later step.

## Precedent-mediated historical method is not automatically Originalism

A respondent can use founding-era public meaning because current Supreme Court doctrine treats history as a required interpretive constraint without independently accepting Originalism as the theory that makes constitutional meaning legally binding. D-ORI7 distinguishes those positions when historical Time answers coexist with unusually strong precedent or an eclectic interpretive method. Rejection of independent Originalist authority prevents the Originalist noun from winning merely from doctrinal compliance.

## False-match rule

Thinker matching is conservative. Numerical closeness is only the start.

A result must not hand someone a thinker when the respondent rejects the proposition that makes that thinker distinctive. Required traits and anti-traits operate as eligibility/falsification checks. A valid classification returns one thinker unit. Close matches and incomplete evidence reduce confidence; response-quality safeguards can withhold the personalized classification. No co-champions are introduced.

This is particularly important where the eight axes compress disputes that are large in substantive philosophy but outside LAWvalues' subject. The quiz does not add separate dimensions for capabilities, hierarchy/domination, distributive justice, equality theory, or political ideology merely to separate people who happen to share adjudicative methods.

## Calibration

The current locations and thresholds are theory-derived prototypes. Pilot responses should later calibrate:

- axis targets and weights;
- specialist gates;
- identity thresholds;
- diagnostic routing frequency;
- ambiguity margins;
- catch-all frequency.

Calibration may move numbers. It should not erase the structural rules above without an explicit redesign.


## Calibrated primary-fixation safeguards (October 2026)

The adjudicative identity must reflect the respondent's strongest well-supported judicial commitment, not merely whichever niche diagnostic carries the highest fixed priority. A supported specialist answer is affirmative subtype evidence, but strong contradictory parent-axis evidence can disqualify it. All candidate identities therefore face a family-appropriate axis alignment and competing-salience penalty before ranking. Descriptive Legal Realism does not displace a clearly stronger normative identity. These coefficients are theory-derived safeguards, not fitted population statistics. Every valid result must display a modifier, real noun, residual phrase, and one champion unit. When secondary evidence cannot justify a specific residual phrase, the neutral mixed-profile phrase is used instead of manufacturing a jurisprudential opinion. Quality-control failures still withhold personalized classifications.

## Version 0.8 acceptance checks

The approved modifier and phrase inventories are immutable in this revision. Tests verify exact catalog hashes, prevent the subtype from being repeated as the residual phrase, and preserve legacy stored IDs. New diagnostic-priority answers never move displayed axes and cannot establish doctrinal compatibility by themselves. Subtype exposure tests hold the core profile fixed and ensure that greater precision does not automatically become primary importance. Adaptive paths remain at or below 100 questions, preserve meaningful specialist reachability, and distinguish skipped priority questions from neutral answers.

Whole-profile explanations are a separate evidence-based reading of the response package. A shared title or champion does not require identical prose. New integrated narrative routes preserve both paragraphs through fresh-session share links, while old saved route pairs remain readable. The finite fixtures and regression grids are software and theory-consistency checks, not empirical validation of people or population proportions.
