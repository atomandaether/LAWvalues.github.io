The dimensions

Eight disagreements that are often collapsed into one

A person can be historically oriented but skeptical of precedent, text-bound but comfortable with systemic remedies, or counter-majoritarian while preferring rules. LAWvalues records those combinations rather than forcing them into a single left–right label.

Instrument design

Transparent by construction

Research-grounded

Items operationalize recurring disputes in the legal literature and doctrine, including Hart's park examples, originalism and living constitutionalism, stare decisis, structural reform, judicial review, separation of powers, and rules versus standards.

Dimension-specific

Each question changes exactly one score. This prevents the instrument itself from manufacturing correlations among otherwise distinct jurisprudential commitments.

Calibration-ready

The item bank, scoring formula, sources, and automated structural audit are public. Jurist and school-of-thought matches remain disabled until pilot data can support them.
