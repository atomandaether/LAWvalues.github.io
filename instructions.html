Before you begin

Answer for your method, not your preferred winner.

The instrument asks what courts and legal institutions should generally do. Treat factual assumptions in each prompt as given and answer the institutional question presented.

1

Use your considered default

Choose the answer that best describes your general legal approach, even when you can imagine exceptions. Do not reverse your method because one example happens to favor a political result you like.

2

Distinguish moderation from uncertainty

“Neither agree nor disagree” is a substantive middle answer. “Not sure / skip” means you do not have a view; skipped items are excluded from that axis's denominator.

Strongly disagree Disagree Slightly disagree Neither Slightly agree Agree Strongly agree
3

Expect a profile, not a verdict

Each question affects one of eight separate scores. The results page shows the full pattern and its strongest commitments. It does not yet assign a jurist, movement, or archetype label.

4

Your answers stay in the browser

Progress is saved only in this tab. The shareable results link contains the eight final scores and item-coverage counts, not the individual answers.
