# Institutional structure / doctrinal form / remedies engine

This file records the locked architecture for the final substantive LAWvalues zone. Public wording can change later without changing this taxonomy.

## Public nouns

This block adds no new public nouns beyond the three already justified by the instrument:

- **Formalist**
- **Functionalist**
- **Proportionalist**

Remedies deliberately do **not** receive a public noun.

## Formalist

Formalist may be the primary noun in either of two genuinely jurisprudential forms:

- **structural Formalism** — categorical institutional assignment of governmental power and prescribed constitutional procedure;
- **rule Formalism** — rules deliberately constrain decision-makers even when a better all-things-considered outcome is available.

### Locked champions

1. **Gary Lawson** — hard structural Formalism / nondelegation. Originalist Time is not part of his matching vector because it is incidental to why the Formalist champion is useful.
2. **Frederick Schauer** — rule Formalism / anti-discretion. Dominant Rules evidence can now generate the Formalist noun directly.
3. **Steven Calabresi / Saikrishna Prakash** — unitary-executive Formalism. E2 supplies the distinctive presidential-control proposition; a direct specificity tie-break lets this unit beat generic Lawson when that proposition is present.

Scalia remains cross-cutting when Originalism or Textualism is the respondent's stronger primary fixation. Kathleen Sullivan remains rules-versus-standards lineage.

## Functionalist

Functionalism remains the established separation-of-powers noun centered on workability, accountability, checks, and tolerated institutional overlap.

4. **Peter Strauss** — locked default champion.

Breyer and Justice White remain related judicial exemplars rather than additional units.

## Proportionalist

Generic balancing is not enough. **D-PROP1** must affirm the structured proportionality sequence.

5. **Robert Alexy** — Higher-law / nonpositivist, principles-heavy Proportionalism.
6. **Aharon Barak** — Purpose-led, purposive and strongly rights-protective Proportionalism.

The title layer preserves:
- **Higher-law Proportionalist** for Alexy-like profiles;
- **Purpose-led Proportionalist** for Barak-like profiles.

D-PROP1 is high priority only when Form is extremely balancing-oriented and either nonpositive Source or purposive/rights-protective evidence makes structured proportionality genuinely plausible. This prevents proportionality diagnostics from consuming slots for ordinary pragmatists.

## Remedies and the single champion slot

Remedies remain a cross-cutting characteristic shown through the title phrase and result explanation. There is no "Remedialist" noun and no secondary/remedial champion slot.

### Selectable

7. **Owen Fiss** — the only current cross-noun remedies exception. He can enter the global champion competition only if systemic Remedies are extremely salient, structural reform and continuing supervision are both strong, and the judicial-role profile is strongly counter-majoritarian. Fiss may displace a noun-compatible champion only if no strong compatible champion exists or Remedies are materially more distinctive than every other axis.

## One champion rule

Every valid classified result receives **exactly one champion unit**.

The ordinary process remains noun-conditioned: primary-compatible champions receive the strongest advantage, cross-cutting-compatible champions remain eligible, and incompatible figures are normally excluded. Specific affirmative evidence resolves ties toward the narrower well-supported unit.

Fiss is the only current cross-noun exception and is subject to the extreme whole-profile gate above.

Close matches reduce confidence but never produce co-champions. If an otherwise valid result has no compatible selectable profile at all, the engine uses the best globally fitting eligible profile rather than leaving the champion slot empty. Response-quality/incoherence overrides still withhold thinker matching.
