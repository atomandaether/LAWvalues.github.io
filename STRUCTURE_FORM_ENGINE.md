# Institutional structure / doctrinal form / remedies engine

This file records the locked architecture for the final substantive LAWvalues zone. Public wording can change later without changing this taxonomy.

## Public nouns

This block adds no new public nouns beyond the three already justified by the instrument:

- **Formalist**
- **Functionalist**
- **Proportionalist**

Remedies deliberately do **not** receive a public noun.

## Formalist

Formalist may be the primary noun in either of two genuinely jurisprudential forms:

- **structural Formalism** — categorical institutional assignment of governmental power and prescribed constitutional procedure;
- **rule Formalism** — rules deliberately constrain decision-makers even when a better all-things-considered outcome is available.

### Locked champions

1. **Gary Lawson** — hard structural Formalism / nondelegation. Originalist Time is not part of his matching vector because it is incidental to why the Formalist champion is useful.
2. **Frederick Schauer** — rule Formalism / anti-discretion. Dominant Rules evidence can now generate the Formalist noun directly.
3. **Steven Calabresi / Saikrishna Prakash** — unitary-executive Formalism. E2 supplies the distinctive presidential-control proposition; a direct specificity tie-break lets this unit beat generic Lawson when that proposition is present.

Scalia remains cross-cutting when Originalism or Textualism is the respondent's stronger primary fixation. Kathleen Sullivan remains rules-versus-standards lineage.

## Functionalist

Functionalism remains the established separation-of-powers noun centered on workability, accountability, checks, and tolerated institutional overlap.

4. **Peter Strauss** — locked default champion.

Breyer and Justice White remain related judicial exemplars rather than additional units.

## Proportionalist

Generic balancing is not enough. **D-PROP1** must affirm the structured proportionality sequence.

5. **Robert Alexy** — non-positivist, principles-heavy Proportionalism.
6. **Aharon Barak** — purposive and strongly rights-protective Proportionalism.

The title layer preserves:
- **Non-Positivist Proportionalist** for Alexy-like profiles;
- **Purposivist Proportionalist** for Barak-like profiles.

When Purposivism rather than Proportionalism is the winning noun, a confirmed Barak-like proportionality commitment can surface reciprocally as **Proportionalist Purposivist**.

D-PROP1 is high priority only when Form is extremely balancing-oriented and either nonpositive Source or purposive/rights-protective evidence makes structured proportionality genuinely plausible. This prevents proportionality diagnostics from consuming slots for ordinary pragmatists.

## Remedies and the single champion slot

Remedies remain a cross-cutting characteristic shown through the title phrase and result explanation. There is no "Remedialist" noun and no secondary/remedial champion slot.

### Selectable

7. **Owen Fiss** — cross-noun systemic-remedies exception. He can enter the global champion competition only if systemic Remedies are extremely salient, structural reform and continuing supervision are both strong, and the judicial-role profile is strongly counter-majoritarian.

8. **P. N. Bhagwati / V. R. Krishna Iyer** — retained because social-action/PIL judging is not merely a remedies preference: it combines procedural-access innovation, systemic relief, strong judicial role, and an open social/purposive method. D-PIL1 remains required.

9. **Samuel Bray** — the opposite-pole cross-noun exception. Extremely salient party-specific Remedies may unlock **D-BRAY1**; only affirmative traditional-equity reasoning allows Bray to displace the ordinary noun-compatible champion. Narrow remedies alone are insufficient.

10. **Henry Monaghan** — champion-level constitutional-common-law specialist. Strong prophylactic-remedy evidence may unlock **D-MON1**; only agreement that implementing doctrine can exceed yet remain legally distinct from the constitutional minimum makes Monaghan eligible beneath compatible Positivist, Legal Process, Common-Law, or Mixed results.

### Lineage / explanation only

The following never take the user's single champion slot:

- **Abram Chayes** — public-law-litigation lineage closely overlapping Fiss.
- **Mila Sohoni / Amanda Frost** — nationwide / universal-relief lineage.
- **Daryl Levinson** — remedial equilibration, whose defining feedback mechanism is not directly measured.

Their ideas remain useful in result explanations and further-reading material.

## One champion rule

Every valid classified result receives **exactly one champion unit**.

The ordinary process remains noun-conditioned: primary-compatible champions receive the strongest advantage, cross-cutting-compatible champions remain eligible, and incompatible figures are normally excluded. Specific affirmative evidence resolves ties toward the narrower well-supported unit.

Fiss and Bray are the two global cross-noun remedies exceptions, each subject to an extreme whole-profile gate. Monaghan remains noun-compatible/cross-cutting rather than globally eligible.

Close matches reduce confidence but never produce co-champions. If an otherwise valid result has no compatible selectable profile at all, the engine uses the best globally fitting eligible profile rather than leaving the champion slot empty. Response-quality/incoherence overrides still withhold thinker matching.

## Theory-based Separation diagnostics (0.7 Beta)

Four conditional diagnostics, D-SEP1–D-SEP4, distinguish theories of presidential direction of executive officers, independent judicial construction of delegated statutory authority, nondelegation despite clear legislative authorization, and the constitutional limits on administrative adjudication. They use no new public axis. When actually asked, they carry modest Separation-axis weight in addition to narrower champion evidence; adaptive routing itself still uses the 88-core scores, so the follow-ups refine rather than recursively determine their own eligibility. They are routed by the strength and relevance of an existing structural judicial philosophy and supply narrower champion signals for Lawson, Calabresi/Prakash, Peter Strauss and appropriate Textualist/Purposivist profiles. The 88-core + up-to-12-adaptive allocation retains the 100-response limit. All four must be reachable on tested Formalist/Functionalist paths.
