# Champion result cards

The existing champion line remains between the result title and respondent portrait. A compact, network-styled card now adds a single-sentence tagline and a short paragraph combining the thinker's biography/contribution with the respondent's evidenced connection.

## Content and boundaries

`champion_content.js` contains individually authored entries for all 83 selectable profile units. Existing grouped units remain grouped. Every entry records claim-bearing institutional, author, publisher, or scholarly sources. Research used available primary text, author abstracts, institutional biographies, publisher descriptions, and specifically attributed scholarly explanation; full books and every linked document were not read in full. Some inaccessible full PDFs were checked through indexed claim-bearing excerpts. `profiles.js`, identities, modifiers, scoring, champion selection, and the respondent's separate opening/context are unchanged.

`champion_cards.js` only presents an already-selected champion. It ranks supported traits by support strength multiplied by an individually authored relevance priority (1–5), with deterministic ties and at most one trait per construct group. It retains up to three distinct constructs for sharing and displays either two complementary clauses or an authored compound affinity. Traits are limited to actual signature/supporting traits with support of at least 0.5 or the classifier's supported-trait ledger. A compound requires independently supporting question IDs in the existing read-only trait-evidence ledger; overlapping aliases or an axis-derived score alone cannot earn it. This presentation ranking does not feed back into scoring or matching. Neither the result title nor the selected thinker can supply missing evidence.

Where no retained trait evidence exists, an axis connection is described only when the score and modeled prototype are on the same side, both at least 18 points from the midpoint, and within 22 points of one another. The copy explicitly limits this to the saved scores. Without a supported trait or aligned modeled axis, the card identifies the saved match without inventing a personal rationale. Each personal connection identifies a particular shared intellectual streak rather than agreement with the thinker's entire worldview. Low-confidence individual clauses retain concise qualification. Ambiguous, absent, withheld, and legacy matches have safe fallbacks.

## Shared replay

New links add `cev=2` and `ce`, a bounded list of at most three allowlisted trait IDs with strengths rounded to three decimals (or `?` for retained support without a magnitude). An optional `ca` names a known authored affinity earned from independent direct evidence. No raw answers or question IDs are added. Reading requires a known evidence version, known selected champion, allowlisted authored traits, valid strengths, and all prerequisites for any saved affinity. Unknown IDs and oversized payloads are ignored. `cev=1` links retain their two-trait compatibility and do not gain compound provenance. Older links remain usable with score-based or saved-match fallback. This is a public, editable result-link format, not an authenticity or psychometric-validation claim. The main result narratives keep their existing independent sharing contract.

## Validation

`audit-champion-cards.mjs` checks complete coverage, source metadata, authored relevance, trait allowlists, independent-item compound gating, deterministic ranking, v1/v2 compatibility, fresh/shared exact replay, absent/negative evidence, legacy and exceptional states, invalid/oversized values, modeled-score agreement and card order. The actual quiz completion/share audit also checks the versioned evidence payload and exact card replay after storage is cleared. Existing full-suite audits remain in place.
