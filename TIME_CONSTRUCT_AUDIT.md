# Instrument 0.9 measurement and compatibility audit

## Two different instruments

The 0.9 instrument revises what several Time questions ask. A stored answer to
an old question is not an answer to its replacement. Tests therefore use two
explicit contexts:

- `audit-whole-profile`, `audit-result-catalog`, and `audit-primary-commitment`
  run historical literal-answer regressions with the preserved 0.8 bank and
  classifier trait mode. Original frozen prompt/answer checks are retained.
- Current question, diagnostic-routing, compatibility, and construct audits
  run the active 0.9 instrument. New temporal controls are authored against
  the revised propositions; historical persona answers are not relabeled as
  new evidence.

The preserved 0.8 bank's complete serialized SHA-256 is
`e9574a33364a5730acd114eda9558887fa8a19e8b309c5f2e1943e5a84f78b41`.
It includes all original question ordering, prompt text, source IDs, keys, and
signals. A separate immutable baseline records all eight original scores for
the ten frozen whole-profile fixtures. It was calculated from the pre-revision
HEAD bank and scorer after checking its bank hash against the working bank hash
recorded before editing.

## Current-instrument controls

The dedicated `audit-time-construct.mjs` checks:

1. Exactly 88 core slots and at most 12 adaptive questions.
2. Distinct missing, skipped, neutral, and genuine 0/100 extreme evidence.
3. T11 authority rejection does not itself assert constitutional evolution.
4. Direct independent-constraint evidence dominates weak historical semantic
   or subtype clues, as does direct evidence of legitimate evolving content.
5. The rewritten independent-authority answer does not silently retain the
   retired early-practice trait.
6. The full historical bank is byte-equivalent after JSON serialization.
7. Invalid current responses are missing evidence, while explicit zero remains neutral.
8. Each axis limits reviewed weak links to 20% of available directional weight.
   Missing-data coverage can still amplify their share of a sparse displayed score;
   this is not a fixed score-point cap.
9. The named Time diagnostic correction removes only eligible weak historical
   contributions, preserves denominators, creates no Living evidence, and logs
   each change. Direct or alternative original-authority evidence blocks it.
10. Temporal answers do not independently establish moral validity or judicial
    deference. Broad remedies do not establish supervision or the structural
    subtype. Other narrow executive-control, textualism, and final-authority
    answers cannot silently become broader doctrines.

In the initial revised core, holding all other answers neutral produces these
controls: the direct fixed-meaning pattern is 97; the direct evolving-content
pattern is 1; agreement only with T7/T8/T10 historical subtype/semantic clues is
53; rejecting T11 alone is 50. These are construct controls, not estimates of
real people's legal philosophies or validated psychometric thresholds.

The result-compatibility audit separately checks that current runtime code
preserves historical stored scores and exports, rejects versionless or
mismatched raw-answer reinterpretation, keeps explicit zero valid, and refuses
invalid or missing score values. Current labels require version-compatible
answer evidence or a strictly whitelisted current label identifier.

## Limits

Passing these software checks does not validate the questionnaire as a
psychometric instrument. Wording, directional weights, any diagnostic-based
component adjustments, and label gates still require substantive review.
The historical suites establish reproducibility; they do not establish that
old and new percentages measure the same construct.
