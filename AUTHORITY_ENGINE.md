# Judicial review / constitutional authority classification engine

This file records the locked architecture for the Posture / constitutional-authority result zone. Public explanatory copy can change later without changing this taxonomy.

## Governing rule

The Posture axis measures how constitutional authority is allocated between courts and democratic institutions. A public authority noun should win only when constitutional authority is actually the respondent's primary jurisprudential fixation; strong Source, Method, Time, Separation, or Form theories remain primary when they are more distinctive.

Six public nouns are locked:

- **Political Constitutionalist**
- **Thayerian**
- **Popular Constitutionalist**
- **Departmentalist**
- **Political Process Jurist**
- **Judicial Minimalist**

**Legal Constitutionalist** and **Weak-Form Constitutionalist** are retained only as internal authority descriptors. Strong-form judicial review and weak-form review remain measured, but they are institutional configurations that can coexist with many jurisprudential identities and therefore no longer compete for the public noun slot. **Waldronian** and **Elyan** remain internal/legacy aliases only.

## Public nouns and champions

### Political Constitutionalist
Functioning representative institutions retain the stronger claim to final constitutional authority under reasonable rights disagreement.

1. **Jeremy Waldron / Richard Bellamy** — locked champion unit. Their philosophical rationales differ, but the current instrument does not measure enough to separate them without violating the Alexander/Kaye threshold.

### Thayerian
Courts retain judicial review but invalidate legislation only when a legislative constitutional judgment falls outside the range of reasonable interpretation.

2. **James Bradley Thayer** — canonical champion.

### Popular Constitutionalist
Ultimate constitutional authority remains with the people and democratic constitutional politics rather than becoming the exclusive property of courts.

3. **Larry Kramer** — locked champion.

### Departmentalist
Coordinate governmental branches retain independent good-faith constitutional interpretive authority within their own functions.

4. **Kevin Walsh** — judicial-departmentalism champion. Lincoln and Jefferson remain lineage.

### Political Process Jurist
Ordinary substantive disagreements remain with politics, while courts intervene more aggressively when democratic channels are blocked, distorted, or exclusionary.

5. **John Hart Ely** — default representation-reinforcement champion.
6. **Rosalind Dixon** — Responsive Judicial Review specialist.

### Judicial Minimalist
Narrow, shallow, incompletely theorized adjudication that resolves no more than necessary.

7. **Cass Sunstein** — default modern minimalist.
8. **Alexander Bickel** — Passive-virtues specialist.

## Substantive modifiers

Only two substantive authority modifiers are locked:

- **Responsive Political Process Jurist** — after **D-RESP1**, routes to Rosalind Dixon.
- **Passive-virtues Judicial Minimalist** — direct O7 passive-virtues evidence, routes to Alexander Bickel.

All other public authority nouns use the generic fit modifier. Strong-form and weak-form judicial-review positions remain available in the axis/trait explanation layer without becoming nouns.

## Adaptive routing

The global cap remains twelve adaptive questions.

- **D-MAJ1** tests whether political branches retain independent interpretive authority after Supreme Court interpretation.
- Agreement with **D-MAJ1** immediately routes to **D-MAJ2**.
- **D-MAJ2** separates Popular Constitutionalism from branch-specific Departmentalism.
- **D-WAL1** separates Political Constitutionalism from Thayerian clear-error deference.
- **D-ELY1** establishes Political Process Jurisprudence.
- Agreement with **D-ELY1** immediately routes to **D-RESP1**.
- **D-RESP1** distinguishes Dixon's Responsive Judicial Review from baseline Elyan representation reinforcement.

No additional minimalist diagnostic is needed:
- O6 still measures weak-form/dialogic review as an institutional-design characteristic, but no longer creates a noun.
- O7 directly measures passive virtues.
- R6, R7, R11, and the Remedies axis measure narrow/shallow incremental adjudication.

## Primary-fixation safeguard

Authority identities may not displace an already-established non-authority specialist or a strongly dominant Source, Constraint, Time, Separation, or Form profile. This keeps institutional-authority preferences from swallowing more informative jurisprudential identities and keeps a strongly Originalist respondent from becoming Thayerian merely because they also favor deference.
